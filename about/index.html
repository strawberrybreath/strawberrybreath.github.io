STRAWBERRY BREATH / EDITORIAL NOTE

让她们成为自己故事的主语。Let women be the subjects of their own stories.

一个从女性视角出发的电影与生活文化刊物。An independent journal on cinema and everyday life, seen through women's perspectives.

我们从电影开始，但不只谈电影。我们关心人物如何作出选择，家庭关系怎样塑造一个人，社会规则如何影响女性，也关心一个人怎样在复杂现实里慢慢找到自己的位置。We begin with cinema, but our interests reach beyond it. We ask how people make choices, how family shapes us, how social rules affect women, and how a person finds her place in a complicated world.

我们不要求每个女性角色都勇敢、独立、完美，也不要求每部作品都给出正确答案。我们想留下的是具体的人、真实的矛盾，以及看完作品之后仍值得思考的问题。We do not expect every woman character to be brave, independent, or perfect, nor every film to offer the right answer. We stay with specific people, real contradictions, and questions that remain after the credits.

01

她的影单HER FILM NOTES

以女性角色与女性创作者为入口，重新观看电影。我们关注的不只是她做了什么，也包括她为什么这样选择、她拥有什么资源，又被什么限制。We revisit cinema through women characters and filmmakers. We ask not only what a woman does, but why she chooses it, what resources she has, and what limits her.

每一期围绕作品或主题展开，结合人物关系、关键场景、叙事方式与社会语境。第一期从 Enola Holmes 出发，讨论母女关系、女性成长与自主选择。Each issue explores a film or theme through relationships, key scenes, storytelling, and social context. Our first feature begins with Enola Holmes and considers motherhood, growth, and self-determination.

她的书架HER BOOKSHELF

03

她的主体性HER AGENCY

面对信息洪流与复杂社会议题，练习判断来源、辨认偏见、检视情绪与利益，思考我们为何相信、接受或拒绝某种观点。主体性不是永远正确，而是能说明自己的判断，也愿意在新证据面前修正它。In an information-saturated world, we practise checking sources, recognising bias, examining emotion and interests, and asking why we accept or reject an idea. Agency does not mean always being right; it means explaining our judgement and revising it when evidence changes.

EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES

我们如何观看与书写How we look and write

从作品本身出发Begin with the work itself

关注人物、叙事、镜头、声音与创作者的表达，而不只给角色贴标签。We pay attention to character, narrative, image, sound, and artistic intention rather than reducing characters to labels.

把女性当作完整的人Treat women as whole people

女性可以犹豫、犯错、矛盾，也可以改变主意。她不需要完美，才值得被认真书写。Women can hesitate, fail, contradict themselves, and change their minds. They do not need to be perfect to deserve serious attention.

尊重不同的生活条件Respect different circumstances

自主不是一句轻巧的口号。经济条件、家庭支持和社会环境不同，选择的空间也会不同。Agency is not a simple slogan. Economic resources, family support, and social conditions shape the choices available to each person.

WHY A WOMAN'S LENS

为什么要重新观看Why look again?

很多故事习惯把女性安排在熟悉的位置：母亲、妻子、恋人、受害者，或者帮助其他人物成长的人。她自己的欲望、恐惧、野心和犹豫，反而可能没有被认真追问。Many stories place women in familiar roles: mother, wife, lover, victim, or the person who helps someone else grow. Their own desires, fears, ambitions, and doubts can go unexamined.

从女性视角观看，不是要求每部作品都提供完美榜样，也不是把人物简单分成正确与错误。我们更愿意问：她为什么这样选择？她能决定什么？她被什么规则限制？她的拒绝是否被当回事？故事有没有允许她改变主意？A woman's perspective does not demand a perfect role model or divide characters into right and wrong. We ask why she chooses as she does, what she can decide, which rules constrain her, whether her refusal matters, and whether the story allows her to change her mind.

有时，一部作品的力量不在于角色赢了，而在于她终于能说出自己真正想要什么。作品本身也许并不完美，但它留下的矛盾依然值得讨论。Sometimes a work's power lies not in a character winning, but in her finally naming what she wants. A film may be imperfect and still leave contradictions worth discussing.

这不是标准答案，也不是要求所有女性遵循的成长路线。有人正在离开，有人选择留下，有人仍在考虑。我们希望不同的生活经验都能被认真对待，而不是彼此审判。This is neither a standard answer nor a prescribed path for every woman. Some are leaving, some are staying, and some are still deciding. We want different experiences to be taken seriously rather than judged against one another.

A SMALL REMINDER
你可以爱一个人，也可以与她不同。
你可以听见世界，也可以保留自己的判断。You can love someone and still disagree with her.
You can listen to the world and keep your own judgement.

愿我们都拥有观看世界的目光，也拥有参与决定自己人生的权利。May we all have the vision to see the world and the right to help shape our own lives.

这里是一个人的观影与阅读记录。我从电影出发，记录女性、关系、成长，以及那些还在慢慢想明白的问题。A personal journal of films and reading: women, relationships, growth, and questions I am still learning to understand.
