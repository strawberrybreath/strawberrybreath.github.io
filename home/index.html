ABOUT THE JOURNAL & THE PERSON BEHIND IT

关于这份刊物About This Journal

一个从女性的目光出发，重新观看电影、书籍、关系与生活的私人记录。A personal journal revisiting cinema, books, relationships, and everyday life through women's perspectives.

观看与阅读Watching & Reading

「草莓味口气」是一个慢慢生长的个人文化刊物。我想把看电影、读书时那些没有立刻消失的感受留下来，也把它们放回真实的生活里，重新想一想。Strawberry Breath is a personal cultural journal in progress. It keeps the feelings that stay with me after a film or a book, and brings them back into the texture of everyday life.

在「影视与她」里，我从女性角色、母女关系、女性友谊与成长经验出发；在「书与她」里，我记录阅读带来的触动，以及文字如何帮助我理解人物、关系和世界。In Her Film Notes, I explore women characters, mother-daughter relationships, friendship, and growth. In Her Bookshelf, I reflect on how literature changes the way I understand people and the world.

我想留下什么What I Hope to Keep

我关心女性如何生活、如何选择，如何在亲密关系、社会期待与现实限制之间，一点点辨认自己的愿望。比起替人物总结出标准答案，我更想认真看见她们经历了什么、为什么这样选择，以及哪些矛盾无法轻易化解。I care about how women live and choose, and how they recognize their own desires amid intimacy, social expectations, and real constraints. Rather than prescribing answers, I want to look closely at their experiences, choices, and unresolved contradictions.

每篇文章都从一次具体的观看或阅读开始。我的理解可能不完整，也可能随着新的阅读和生活经验而改变。我希望这里能容纳犹豫、修正和继续思考。Each piece begins with a particular viewing or reading. My understanding may be incomplete and may change with new experiences; this space should leave room for uncertainty, revision, and continued thought.

不是替女性写出标准答案，而是认真看见她们如何生活。Not to write standard answers for women, but to pay attention to how they actually live.
