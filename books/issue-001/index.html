L’amica geniale

她们互相照亮，也互相刺痛：女性友谊不是温柔的童话

这一期是我作为读者 / 观众写下的个人笔记。我想谈的不只是情节如何推进，也包括作品怎样让我重新思考女性的选择、关系与自由。

PERSONAL NOTES · READING · 01

先认识作者：为什么这段女性友谊值得写四本书？

作者与创作背景 · AUTHOR CONTEXT

Elena Ferrante 是笔名，作者长期选择不公开个人身份，并希望读者把注意力放在作品本身。她谈到写作时说，作者会把自己的经验与文学感受写进故事，却未必能完全预见作品最终包含的意义。她也明确谈过自己想描写的女性关系：一段持续一生的友谊，可以同时容纳依赖、反抗、互相支持与背叛。这个背景很重要：Lila 与 Elena 并不是用来证明“女性友谊一定美好”的范例，而是两位会互相影响、比较、嫉妒、爱与伤害的具体的人。来源：Elena Ferrante 官方作品页及访谈；Vogue：关于女性友谊的访谈。

因此，阅读这四部作品时，我更想留意的不是谁比谁更优秀，而是教育、阶级、家庭、婚姻和社会环境怎样改变她们的可能性；也留意她们如何在爱与竞争并存的关系里，一次次重写自己。

ENGLISH · CONTEXT

Elena Ferrante is a pen name; the author has consistently kept her private identity out of the foreground. In public interviews, she has described the Neapolitan novels as an exploration of a lifelong friendship between women—one that can hold dependence, rebellion, support, and betrayal at once. Lila and Elena are not models of perfect sisterhood. They are complicated people shaped by education, class, family, marriage, ambition, and the limits of their world. Read the author’s official work page and interview and the Vogue interview on female friendship.

1. 我为什么会被Lila和Elena吸引

中文 · MY NOTES

我原本以为这会是一部关于两个女孩如何成为好朋友的成长小说，但真正读进去之后，我发现它写的是一种更难被说清楚的关系：羡慕、依赖、竞争、嫉妒、崇拜和怨恨可以同时存在。Lila的聪明像一束刺眼的光，Elena既被她吸引，也被她照出自己的不安。她们不是彼此的完美答案，而是彼此最难绕开的参照物。正是这种不体面的复杂，让她们像真实的人，而不是用来证明“女性友谊很美好”的样板。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

I expected a coming-of-age story about two girls becoming best friends. Instead, it explores a harder relationship: admiration, dependence, competition, envy, devotion, and resentment can coexist. Lila’s brilliance is almost blinding; Elena is drawn to her and unsettled by the insecurities Lila exposes. They are not each other’s perfect answers, but each other’s hardest point of reference. Their unflattering complexity makes them feel human rather than examples meant to prove that female friendship is always beautiful.

2. 天赋和努力为什么会被讲成命运

中文 · MY NOTES

Lila似乎天生就能看穿事物，Elena则努力学习、争取老师认可，把成绩当成离开街区的可能。她们的差异很容易被解释成“一个天才，一个普通人”，但才能从来不是在真空里生长的。家庭经济条件、女孩是否被允许继续上学、照料责任和街区的暴力，都影响一个人能走到哪里。Elena的努力不该被浪漫化成只要拼命就能成功，Lila的天赋也不意味着她就能自由选择人生。我们是不是太容易把结构性的门槛，包装成某个人不够努力？

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

Lila seems instinctively able to see through things, while Elena studies hard and treats education as a possible route out of the neighborhood. It is tempting to reduce them to “a genius and an ordinary girl,” but talent never grows in a vacuum. Family resources, permission to continue school, care work, and neighborhood violence shape what a person can become. Elena’s effort should not be romanticized as proof that anyone can succeed by trying hard enough; Lila’s talent does not grant her freedom either. Do we too easily turn structural barriers into a story about someone not trying hard enough?

先认真看见一个女人的处境，再判断她的选择。

3. 友谊里的比较，和我们不愿承认的嫉妒

中文 · MY NOTES

我很喜欢Elena Ferrante不急着把嫉妒洗白。Elena会比较自己的外表、成绩、受欢迎程度和未来；她想摆脱Lila的影响，却又无法停止观察她。女性之间的竞争不是天生的本能，而是在资源有限、认可稀缺的环境里被放大的东西：仿佛一个女孩得到机会，就意味着另一个女孩失去机会。意识到这一点并不意味着要否认嫉妒，而是可以问问自己：我真正想要的是什么？我是在追求自己的生活，还是在努力证明我没有输给某个人？

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

I appreciate that Ferrante does not rush to sanitize envy. Elena compares her looks, grades, popularity, and future. She wants to escape Lila’s influence and cannot stop watching her. Competition between women is amplified in environments where resources and recognition feel scarce, as if one girl’s opportunity must mean another girl’s loss. Recognizing this does not require denying envy. It lets me ask: What do I actually want? Am I building a life that feels like mine, or trying to prove I have not lost to someone else?

4. 第一本书给我的启蒙

中文 · MY NOTES

这本书没有教我如何做一个更好的朋友，却让我更诚实地看待友谊。一个人可以爱你，也可能伤害你；你可以欣赏另一个女人，也不必把她当成衡量自己的尺子。女性的成长不只发生在恋爱或婚姻里，也发生在同伴关系、学习机会和对自我价值的判断里。亲近不必抹掉边界，欣赏不必变成自我贬低，而友谊也不需要永远体面才值得被认真书写。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

This book does not teach me how to be a better friend; it makes me more honest about friendship. Someone can love you and still hurt you. You can admire another woman without making her the ruler by which you measure yourself. Women grow not only through romance or marriage, but through friendships, access to education, and the ways they judge their own worth. Closeness need not erase boundaries, admiration need not become self-belittlement, and friendship does not have to be graceful at every moment to deserve serious attention.

写在最后 · A final note

中文

我写下这些感受，不是要把小说变成一堂课，而是因为它让我愿意停下来，重新看一眼自己习以为常的判断。女性不必活成同一种样子；理解她们，也包括允许她们矛盾、犯错、改变，和不按我们期待的方式结束故事。

ENGLISH

I am not trying to turn this novel into a lesson. It has made me pause and reconsider judgments I once took for granted. Women do not have to live in one approved way. Understanding them means allowing them to be contradictory, to make mistakes, to change, and to end their stories in ways we did not expect.
