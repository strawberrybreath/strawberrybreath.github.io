HER BOOKSHELF / ISSUE 04

失踪的孩子

没有简单的胜利：母职、记忆与女性的复杂一生
No Simple Victory: Motherhood, Memory, and a Woman’s Complicated Life

这是我作为二十六岁女性读者写下的个人阅读札记。我不想替小说下定论，而是把人物让我感到复杂、刺痛或共鸣的地方认真写下来。

A personal reading note from a twenty-six-year-old woman. Rather than declaring what the novel means, I stay with the characters’ contradictions and the moments that moved or unsettled me.

STRAWBERRY BREATH · HER BOOKSHELFISSUE 04 / 中文 · ENGLISH

1. 成为母亲之后，仍然是一个完整的人A woman remains whole after becoming a mother

中文 · MY NOTES

第四部让母亲身份、事业、欲望和旧日友谊纠缠得更紧。女性成为母亲，并不意味着她的其他部分自动消失；她仍然可能有野心、嫉妒、愤怒和欲望，也可能对孩子感到疲惫和矛盾。文学常把母亲写成无私的照料者，仿佛母爱应该让一个女人不再需要自己。费兰特拒绝这种整洁的神话，让我们看见母职既有爱，也有劳动、限制和无法轻易说出口的感受。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

The fourth volume entangles motherhood, work, desire, and old friendship. Becoming a mother does not make the rest of a woman disappear. She may still have ambition, envy, anger, and desire, and may feel exhausted or conflicted about her children. Literature often turns mothers into selfless caregivers, as if motherhood should erase their own needs. Ferrante refuses this neat myth, showing that motherhood contains love, labor, constraint, and feelings that are not easy to admit.

2. 女性之间的关系会留下很长的回声Relationships between women leave long echoes

中文 · MY NOTES

莉拉和埃莱娜的关系贯穿一生，却从来不是一条稳定、温柔的直线。她们互相影响、互相伤害，也在对方身上看见自己不愿面对的部分。读到最后，我不再期待她们必须和解，或给这段关系一个让人安心的结论。现实里的关系有时没有漂亮的收束：有些问题被说开，有些始终没有；有些人离开，有些人留下，但影响仍在。女性主义允许我们认真看见女性之间真实的权力、依恋与冲突。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

Lila and Elena’s relationship lasts a lifetime, but it is never a steady, gentle line. They shape and hurt each other, seeing in the other what they struggle to face in themselves. By the end, I no longer expect them to reconcile or deliver a reassuring conclusion. Real relationships do not always close neatly. Feminism allows us to look honestly at power, attachment, and conflict between women.

先认真看见一个女人的处境，再判断她的选择。See a woman’s circumstances clearly before judging her choices.

3. 谁有权决定一个女人如何被记住Who gets to decide how a woman is remembered?

中文 · MY NOTES

书名里的“失踪”让我想到女性如何被记忆、被叙述。一个女人的生活很容易被概括成某种角色：谁的妻子、谁的母亲、谁的朋友、谁的成功案例或失败例子。莉拉难以被任何单一叙述完全抓住，她的消失也让埃莱娜不得不重新面对自己讲述这段故事的方式。写作不是透明记录；叙述者会选择、遗漏、解释，也可能误读。读者不必把叙述者的版本当成唯一真相。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

The disappearance in the title makes me think about how women are remembered and narrated. A woman’s life can be reduced to a role: someone’s wife, mother, friend, success story, or failure. Lila resists being contained by any single account, and her disappearance forces Elena to confront how she has told their story. Writing is not transparent; narrators select, omit, interpret, and sometimes misunderstand. Readers should not treat one version as the only truth.

4. 读完四本，我想留下的不是答案After four books, I am left with questions, not answers

中文 · MY NOTES

这四本书让我看到女性生活里那些不够体面、却真实存在的部分：想被爱又害怕失去自由，想成功又嫉妒别人，爱孩子却也怀念曾经属于自己的时间，渴望逃离却发现世界处处有门槛。我不觉得这些矛盾让女性显得软弱，反而觉得它们让人物拥有完整的人性。作为二十六岁的读者，我想带走的不是一套如何活得正确的规则，而是一种更耐心的目光：少一点急着评判，多一点追问她身处什么条件、真正想要什么，以及有没有机会自己决定。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

These four books reveal parts of women’s lives that are not always graceful but are real: wanting love while fearing the loss of freedom, wanting success while envying others, loving children while missing time that once belonged to oneself, wanting to escape and finding barriers everywhere. These contradictions do not make women weak; they give the characters their full humanity. As a twenty-six-year-old reader, I want not a rulebook, but a more patient way of looking: less instant judgment, more curiosity about a woman’s circumstances, desires, and freedom to decide.

写在最后 · A final note

中文

我写下这些感受，不是要把小说变成一堂课，而是因为它让我停下来，重新看一眼自己习以为常的判断。女性不必活成同一种样子；理解她们，也包括允许她们矛盾、犯错、改变，和不按我们期待的方式结束故事。

ENGLISH

I am not trying to turn this novel into a lesson. It has made me pause and reconsider judgments I once took for granted. Women do not have to live in one approved way. Understanding them means allowing them to be contradictory, to make mistakes, to change, and to end their stories in ways we did not expect.
