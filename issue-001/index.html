HER FILM NOTES / ISSUE 01

Enola Holmes

母亲、女儿，以及成为自己的路Mothers, Daughters, and the Path to Becoming Oneself

我们熟悉 Sherlock Holmes，却未必认识他的妹妹 Enola。比起再讲一遍她如何破案，这次我更想聊聊她和母亲 Eudoria：一个女孩如何带着母亲留下的东西长大，又如何在爱与不解之间，慢慢成为自己。We know Sherlock Holmes, but may know less about his younger sister, Enola. Rather than retelling how she solves cases, this feature looks at Enola and her mother, Eudoria: how a young woman grows with what her mother leaves behind, and becomes herself amid love and misunderstanding.

这篇文章把 Enola Holmes、Enola Holmes 2 与 Enola Holmes 3 放在一起，观察人物关系与主题如何变化。宣传素材不会被误称为剧情证据，作品也不会被当成女性主义的完整答案。This feature considers Enola Holmes, Enola Holmes 2, and Enola Holmes 3 together, tracing how their relationships and themes evolve. Promotional imagery is not treated as narrative evidence, and the films are not presented as complete answers to feminism.

01 / THE FILMS

三部电影：从寻找母亲，到定义自己Three Films: From Finding Her Mother to Defining Herself

三部电影围绕 Enola Holmes 展开：她从母亲失踪后的独自出走开始，逐步成为独立办案的年轻侦探；之后，她面对女性劳动者的处境、制度性的不公，以及亲密关系与个人目标之间的张力。以下按上映顺序列出官方海报与故事简介。The trilogy follows Enola Holmes from her search for her missing mother to her work as an independent young detective. Along the way, she encounters the conditions faced by working women, institutional injustice, and the tension between intimacy and personal ambition. The films are presented below in release order with their official posters and story introductions.

FILM 01 · 2020

Enola Holmes

1884年的英格兰，Enola在十六岁生日当天发现母亲 Eudoria 失踪。哥哥们打算把她送进培养“淑女”的寄宿学校，她却选择逃往伦敦寻找母亲，并意外卷入一场可能影响政治局势的阴谋。第一部把母女关系、女性自主与侦探冒险放在同一条成长线上。In 1884 England, Enola discovers that her mother, Eudoria, has disappeared on her sixteenth birthday. When her brothers plan to send her to a finishing school, she escapes to London to find her mother and becomes entangled in a conspiracy with political consequences. The first film brings mother-daughter ties, female agency, and detective adventure together.

FILM 02 · 2022

Enola Holmes 2

成为职业侦探后，Enola接到第一桩正式委托：寻找一名失踪火柴女工的妹妹。案件将她带进工厂、音乐厅与上流社会，也让她看见年轻女性在劳动与制度中的脆弱处境。调查过程中，她与母亲重逢，母女之间的爱、分歧与各自的行动再次成为故事的重要部分。Now a detective-for-hire, Enola takes her first official case: finding the missing sister of a young match factory worker. The investigation leads through factories, music halls, and high society, exposing the precarious position of working women. She also reunites with her mother, bringing their love, disagreements, and independent actions back to the centre of the story.

FILM 03 · 2026

Enola Holmes 3

Enola前往Malta，新的案件与她对未来关系的思考交织在一起。当婚姻、职业目标与个人愿望同时摆在面前，她必须继续判断自己想要怎样的生活。第三部把成长推进到新的阶段：独立不只是离开他人的安排，也是在亲密关系中保有选择与自我认识。In Malta, a new case intersects with Enola’s thoughts about her future relationship. With marriage, professional goals, and personal desires all in play, she must decide what kind of life she wants. The third film moves her growth into a new phase: independence means not only escaping others’ plans, but retaining choice and self-knowledge within intimacy.

05 / TONE & LIMITS

轻快并不等于浅薄A light touch is not the same as a shallow one

喜剧感、快速推进的冒险和 Enola 与观众直接交流的方式，让观众可以轻松进入故事。轻快的形式并不会自动削弱议题；有时它反而让关于权力、性别角色和社会期待的问题更容易被看见。Comedy, fast-moving adventure, and Enola’s direct address to the audience make the story easy to enter. A light form does not automatically weaken its themes; at times, it can make questions of power, gender roles, and social expectation easier to see.

但我们不必把它当成女性主义的完整答案。它仍是一部商业类型片，有自己的叙事取舍与浪漫化表达。Enola的好奇心、行动力，以及她与母亲之间并不简单的关系，足够成为继续思考的起点。Still, the films are not complete answers to feminism. They remain commercial genre films, with narrative compromises and romanticised elements. Enola’s curiosity, initiative, and complicated relationship with her mother offer a starting point for further thought.

06 / CLOSING NOTE

写在最后A closing note

我喜欢 Enola 身上那种机敏、幽默和不愿轻易服从的劲头，也喜欢这个故事没有要求她必须在母亲、爱情、家庭和自我之间交出一个唯一正确的答案。I admire Enola’s wit, humour, and reluctance to obey without question. I also value that the story does not demand one perfect answer from her about her mother, romance, family, or herself.

或许长大不是终于变成一个无所不能的人，而是慢慢知道：我可以爱我的母亲，也可以与她不同；我可以听取别人的意见，也可以自己做决定；我可以在寻找答案的路上不断改变。Perhaps growing up does not mean becoming capable of everything. It means learning that I can love my mother and still differ from her; listen to others and still make my own decisions; and keep changing as I search for answers.

你和母亲之间，有没有一件事，是长大以后才开始理解、但仍然不完全认同的？Is there something about your mother that you began to understand as you grew older, but still do not fully agree with?
