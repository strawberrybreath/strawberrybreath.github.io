Enola Holmes

我喜欢她的，不只是聪明：一个女孩怎样开始相信自己
What I Love About Enola Is Not Just Her Brilliance

《Enola Holmes》是一部轻快的侦探冒险电影，也借 Enola 的成长提出关于教育、母女关系与女性自主的问题。这篇文章从具体情节出发，记录我对这些问题的理解。

A personal essay on Enola Holmes, exploring how its mystery and coming-of-age story raise questions about education, mother-daughter relationships, and women’s autonomy.

01. 她的名字里有“独自”，但她并不是从孤独开始的Her Name Means “Alone,” but Her Story Begins with Belonging

中文 · MY NOTES

《Enola Holmes》开场时，Enola 直接对观众说话。她解释自己的名字倒过来就是 alone——独自一人。这个双关很轻巧，像是电影提前给人物贴上的标签；但当我们真正看见她与 Eudoria 相处的片段，就会发现她并不是在孤独中长大的。母亲陪她读书、运动、做实验，也鼓励她去碰触那些通常不被认为属于淑女教育的事。她的成长环境并不符合维多利亚时代对女孩的标准，却给了她一种更重要的东西：她可以主动认识世界，而不是只等待世界来安排她。

因此，母亲离开时，Enola 失去的不只是一个照顾自己的人。她失去了唯一熟悉的生活秩序，也突然被推入一个早已为她准备好答案的社会。Mycroft 看到的是一个需要被管教、被送去寄宿学校的女孩；他把礼仪、衣着和“体面”当作修复她的办法。Enola 感受到的却是另一种危险：如果她接受这些安排，她可能会慢慢学会不再相信自己的判断。电影里的束腰、帽子、手套和仪态训练看起来带有喜剧效果，但它们并不只是复古装饰。它们让身体成为社会规训的现场——女孩应该怎样坐、怎样走、怎样说话，甚至怎样占据空间，都有人替她规定。

我喜欢 Enola 并不是因为她永远正确，而是因为她没有把“不合适”误认为“我有问题”。她会笨拙，会害怕，也会在陌生环境里犯错；她的聪明不是天生无所不能，而是她愿意观察、试错，再重新判断。电影用打破第四面墙的方式让她直接向观众解释自己，有时确实显得过于频繁，也会把悬疑情节变得轻巧。但这种叙述方式也有它的意义：Enola 不只是被别人观察的对象，她有机会亲自解释发生在自己身上的事。她不必等 Sherlock 或 Mycroft 替她定义她是谁。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

At the beginning of Enola Holmes, the heroine tells us that her name spells “alone” backwards. It is a neat joke, almost a label placed on her before the story begins. Yet the memories of her life with Eudoria show that Enola did not grow up in isolation. Her mother taught her to read, exercise, experiment, and explore subjects that had little to do with the conventional education of a Victorian lady. Her childhood did not fit society’s expectations, but it gave her something more valuable: permission to investigate the world rather than wait for the world to define her.

When Eudoria disappears, Enola loses more than a caretaker. She loses the only familiar order in her life and is pushed into a society that already has answers prepared for her. Mycroft sees a girl who must be disciplined and sent away to school. Manners, clothes, and respectability become his proposed remedies. Enola senses a different danger: if she accepts these arrangements, she may gradually stop trusting her own judgment. The corset, gloves, hat, and posture lessons are played for comedy, but they also show how the body becomes a site of social control. Someone has decided how a girl should sit, walk, speak, and occupy space.

I admire Enola not because she is always right, but because she does not mistake being called “improper” for being defective. She is awkward and frightened, and she makes mistakes. Her intelligence is not omnipotence; it is a willingness to observe, test, and reconsider. The frequent fourth-wall breaks can interrupt the mystery, yet they also give Enola something rare: the chance to interpret her own experience instead of waiting for Sherlock or Mycroft to tell the world who she is.

02. 母亲的离开，既是礼物，也是伤口A Mother’s Departure Can Be Both a Gift and a Wound

中文 · MY NOTES

这部电影最值得停下来想的，不是 Eudoria 究竟是不是一个“好母亲”，而是我们为什么总想用一个简单答案来判断她。她离开女儿去参与更广泛的政治行动，显然不是为了惩罚 Enola；她相信女性不应该只在家庭内部生活，也不应该把所有才智都用来维持一个舒适、安稳、被社会认可的家。她把书、谜题和独立思考的能力留给女儿，某种意义上是在准备她走出家门之后仍能照顾自己。

但教育不能自动抵消离别带来的痛苦。Enola 不知道母亲去了哪里，也不知道自己是否被抛弃。她的困惑不是思想不够成熟，而是一个孩子有权提出的情感问题。电影并没有把 Eudoria 的选择写成毫无代价的英雄行为：她的事业有其正当性，女儿的受伤也同样真实。两者并不互相取消。一个女人可以拥有家庭以外的使命，但她的使命不会让亲密关系中的责任凭空消失；一个女儿可以理解母亲追求自由的理由，同时仍然需要解释、陪伴和道歉。

这也是我觉得这部电影比单纯的“女孩子要独立”更有意思的地方。女性主义不应该要求女性成为另一种完美角色——既要无私地照顾家庭，又要无所畏惧地改变世界，还不能让任何人失望。Eudoria 不需要因为她是母亲就放弃自己的政治理想；但她也不应该仅仅因为理想崇高，就被免除所有关系责任。Enola 同样不必立刻把母亲的选择理解成一种祝福。爱一个人，不等于必须马上原谅她；理解她的处境，也不意味着自己的感受就不重要。

影片最后让母女关系重新靠近，但并没有要求 Enola 变成一个只会追随母亲脚步的人。她接过的不是一份必须照着执行的使命，而是一种继续追问的能力。对我来说，这比把母亲塑造成完美的女性榜样更可信：母女之间真正有价值的传承，不是复制彼此的人生，而是允许下一代用自己的方式作出选择。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

The most interesting question is not whether Eudoria is a “good mother,” but why we want such a simple verdict. She leaves to take part in a wider political struggle because she believes women should not be confined to the household or required to spend all their intelligence maintaining a respectable home. The books, puzzles, and habits of independent thought she gives Enola are preparations for a life beyond the family.

But education cannot cancel the pain of being left behind. Enola does not know where her mother has gone or whether she has been abandoned. This confusion is not a failure of maturity; it is an emotional question a child has every right to ask. The film does not make Eudoria’s decision a cost-free heroic act. Her cause may be justified, and her daughter’s hurt may be real. Neither truth erases the other. A woman can have a mission beyond family, but that mission does not make relational responsibilities disappear. A daughter can understand her mother’s reasons and still need explanation, presence, and an apology.

This is where the film becomes more interesting than a simple slogan about independence. Feminism should not demand a new, impossible ideal: a woman must care for everyone, change the world fearlessly, and never disappoint anyone. Eudoria should not have to abandon her politics because she is a mother; nor should a noble cause exempt her from every consequence of her choices. Enola does not have to interpret her mother’s departure as a blessing immediately. Loving someone does not require instant forgiveness, and understanding another person’s circumstances does not make our own feelings irrelevant.

Their eventual reconnection does not turn Enola into a follower. What she inherits is not a mission she must copy, but the ability to keep asking questions. That feels more honest than presenting a mother as a flawless feminist model: a meaningful inheritance between generations allows the next person to choose a different life.

03. 破案之外：谁有资格决定一个女孩的未来？Beyond the Mystery: Who Gets to Decide a Girl’s Future?

中文 · MY NOTES

寻找母亲的线索，与 Tewkesbury 的案件并行展开。表面上看，这是两条不同的冒险线；但它们都在问一个问题：一个年轻女性能否拥有自己的判断，并据此行动？当 Enola 发现 Tewkesbury 可能遭遇危险，她没有把他当成一个等待被拯救的贵族少年，而是开始调查那些大人不愿认真对待的线索。后来她面对的也不只是某个坏人的阴谋，而是一个社会如何把“合适的婚姻”“体面的女性”和政治权力捆在一起。

影片对维多利亚时代的限制做了轻快处理：逃跑、伪装、打斗和谜题让故事始终保持娱乐性。这种轻快有优点，它让历史议题更容易进入大众文化；但也有局限。Enola 的勇敢与机敏，并不能代表当时所有女性都拥有同样的逃离条件。她有教育、有家庭资源，也有足够的身体能力和叙事主导权。对于贫穷、受雇、无法自由旅行的女性而言，“只要勇敢就能选择自己的人生”远远不够。

因此，我不想把 Enola 当作所有女性都应该模仿的标准答案。她的故事更像一扇门：它让我们看见限制是如何运作的，也让我们继续追问，现实里是谁在承担限制的代价。她能靠个人能力打开一条路，这当然令人振奋；但真正的社会改变还需要法律、资源、教育和其他人的共同参与。个人的勇敢值得赞赏，却不能成为制度不必改变的借口。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

The search for Eudoria runs alongside the mystery involving Tewkesbury. On the surface, these are separate adventures, but both ask whether a young woman can trust her own judgment and act on it. Enola does not treat Tewkesbury as a helpless aristocratic boy waiting to be rescued; she investigates clues that adults are inclined to dismiss. The danger she confronts is not only an individual villain’s plot, but a society that ties “suitable” marriages and respectable femininity to political power.

The film treats Victorian restrictions lightly through disguises, chases, fights, and puzzles. This tone makes historical questions accessible, but it also has limits. Enola’s courage cannot stand in for every woman’s options at the time. She has education, family resources, physical skills, and narrative control. For a poor working woman who cannot travel freely or leave employment, “be brave and choose your own life” is not enough.

I do not want to treat Enola as a standard every woman should imitate. Her story is a doorway: it reveals how restrictions work and invites us to ask who pays their real-world costs. Her ability to open a path for herself is inspiring, but social change also requires law, resources, education, and collective effort. Individual courage deserves admiration; it should not become an excuse for institutions to remain unchanged.

04. 我喜欢她的，不是“什么都能做到”What I Admire Is Not That She Can Do Everything

中文 · MY NOTES

电影的结尾没有让 Enola 变成一个不再需要任何人的人。她仍然与母亲、兄长、Tewkesbury 以及其他人建立联系，只是她开始争取在这些关系中保留自己的判断。独立不是拒绝帮助，不是把脆弱藏起来，也不是每次都必须赢。更重要的是，一个人可以在关系里被爱、被支持，同时仍然拥有对自己生活的解释权和决定权。

我也能理解为什么有人会觉得这部电影的女性主义表达太直白，甚至像是把观点写在了人物台词里。它有时确实会把复杂的社会议题变成容易理解的口号，悬疑本身也不总是足够精巧。但我仍然喜欢它轻盈的气质：它没有要求女孩先变得完美，才有资格冒险；也没有要求她必须讨人喜欢，才有资格被认真对待。对一个正在学习如何安排自己生活的人来说，这样的提醒已经有价值。自由不是一种性格，而是一种应该被允许拥有的权利。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

The ending does not turn Enola into someone who needs nobody. She continues to build relationships with her mother, brothers, Tewkesbury, and others; she simply learns to retain her own judgment within them. Independence is not refusing help, hiding vulnerability, or winning every time. It means being loved and supported without surrendering the right to interpret and shape one’s own life.

I understand why some viewers find the film’s feminism too direct, as if its ideas are occasionally written straight into the dialogue. It sometimes turns complicated social questions into easy slogans, and the mystery is not always intricate enough. Still, I like its lightness. It does not require a girl to become perfect before she can have an adventure, or universally likable before she deserves to be taken seriously. For anyone learning how to shape a life, that reminder matters. Freedom is not a personality type; it is a right people should be allowed to exercise.
