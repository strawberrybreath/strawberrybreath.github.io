Enola Holmes

我喜欢她的，不只是聪明：一个女孩怎样开始相信自己
What I Love About Enola Is Not Just Her Brilliance

这一期是我重新观看《Enola Holmes》时写下的个人笔记。我想谈的不只是剧情如何推进，也包括它怎样让我想到女性的选择、关系与自由。

A personal note from watching Enola Holmes. I am interested not only in how the plot unfolds, but in how it makes me think about women’s choices, relationships, and freedom.

PERSONAL NOTES · CINEMA · 01
STRAWBERRY BREATH · HER FILM NOTESISSUE 01 / 中文 · ENGLISH

先认识创作者：这部电影为什么从女性视角讲故事？Before the story: the people and ideas behind the film

创作背景 · CREATIVE CONTEXT

Harry Bradbeer 是英国导演，曾执导 Fleabag 与 Killing Eve；他擅长把幽默、矛盾与女性人物的主观经验放在一起。谈到 Enola Holmes 时，他说自己想做一部“realistic but also fun and magical”的电影——既有冒险的轻快，也要真正表达一些东西。对他而言，女性故事并非附着在推理情节上的装饰，而是作品的核心动力。来源：Los Angeles Times 采访。

Millie Bobby Brown 不只是主演，也参与制片。她在采访中说，自己希望做出一部能够鼓励年轻女孩的电影；她与导演围绕“energy, emotion and eccentricity”确立了创作基调。故事的另一层源头是 Nancy Springer 的青少年推理小说系列：它把 Sherlock Holmes 的世界交给了一个有自己判断力、会犯错、也会重新选择的年轻女性。来源：British Vogue 采访。

ENGLISH · CONTEXT

Director Harry Bradbeer, known for Fleabag and Killing Eve, wanted Enola Holmes to be “realistic but also fun and magical.” The film’s lead, Millie Bobby Brown, also served as a producer and said she wanted to make something that would inspire young girls. The story adapts Nancy Springer’s young-adult mystery novels, giving a young woman the authority to investigate, interpret, and choose her own future. Read the Los Angeles Times interview and British Vogue interview.

“There are two paths you can take, Enola. Yours or the path others choose for you.”
“Our future is up to us.”Eudoria Holmes · Enola Holmes (2020) · Dialogue reference

1. 一个不愿意被安排的女孩A girl who refuses to be arranged

中文 · MY NOTES

我喜欢Enola身上那种有点莽撞、甚至不太讨喜的生命力。她不总是礼貌，不擅长把每句话说得圆滑，也不愿意为了让别人舒服而缩小自己。她的聪明不是一种安静、被允许展示的天赋，而是会让既有秩序感到麻烦的东西。她观察、推理、奔跑，也会害怕和犯错。正因为她不是一个永远正确的“女英雄”，她的成长才显得可信。对我来说，女性独立并不是把自己训练成无所不能的人，而是逐渐相信：我可以自己判断，也可以在判断失误后重新来过。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

I love Enola’s unruly vitality: she can be awkward, impolite, and far from universally likable. She refuses to make herself smaller just to keep other people comfortable. Her intelligence unsettles an established order. She observes, reasons, runs, gets frightened, and makes mistakes. Because she is not an infallible heroine, her growth feels believable. Independence does not mean training myself to be invincible. It means trusting my own judgment—and knowing I can begin again when that judgment fails.

2. 母亲留下的，不是标准答案What her mother leaves her is not an answer key

中文 · MY NOTES

Eudoria的离开，起初很容易被理解成一个母亲把女儿留在了身后。但电影慢慢让我们看到，母亲并没有把女儿塑造成一个等待保护的孩子；她把线索、能力和选择的空间留给了Enola。这个安排并不意味着母亲的做法没有伤害，也不意味着母女之间的爱可以自动免除解释与责任。我反而喜欢它保留了矛盾：母亲有自己的事业和信念，女儿也有权因为被留下而生气。爱不是要求对方永远理解自己，而是承认对方也有独立的感受。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

Eudoria’s departure can initially look like a mother leaving her daughter behind. Gradually, we see that she has not raised Enola to wait for rescue. She leaves her clues, skills, and room to choose. That does not make her decisions harmless, nor does love erase the need for explanation and accountability. I appreciate that the film keeps the contradiction alive: a mother can have her own work and convictions, while a daughter can still feel hurt. Love does not require instant understanding; it requires recognizing the other person’s feelings and life.

我想认真看见一个女人的处境，而不是急着判断她做得对不对。I want to see a woman’s circumstances clearly before judging whether she has done the right thing.

3. 女性主义也发生在日常的小事里Feminism also lives in ordinary details

中文 · MY NOTES

故事把女性的处境放在维多利亚时代的制度里：教育、婚姻、财产和公共发言权都不是每个人天然拥有的。Enola的冒险让这些限制变得具体，而不是一段抽象的历史说明。她拒绝被塞进“合适的淑女”模板，也拒绝把自己的未来交给兄长或追求者决定。我不认为每个女性都必须像Enola一样勇敢外向；更重要的是，她不必用同一种方式证明自己值得被尊重。一个人可以想恋爱，也可以不想；可以温柔，也可以锋利。选择权比某一种固定的女性形象更重要。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

The story places women’s lives inside Victorian structures: education, marriage, property, and public speech were not equally available to everyone. Enola’s adventures make those restrictions tangible. She refuses the template of the “proper young lady” and will not hand her future to a brother or suitor. Not every woman has to be as bold as Enola. What matters is that she should not have to perform one approved kind of womanhood to deserve respect. A woman may want romance or not; she may be gentle or sharp. The right to choose matters more than any single image of a woman.

4. 我从她身上带走的东西What I take away from her

中文 · MY NOTES

这部电影让我想到，成长不是某一天突然变得成熟，而是一次次从别人替我解释世界，走向自己去观察世界。别人可以给建议、给爱、给帮助，但不能代替我生活。Enola的故事轻快、有悬疑感，也会用幽默让严肃的议题不那么沉重；它不是现实里所有女孩都能轻松逃脱限制的证明，却能成为一个入口，让我们继续追问：为什么有些选择总被认为不适合女性？为什么女孩的独立常常要先通过别人的许可？我喜欢它给我的不是完美答案，而是继续提问的勇气。

ENGLISH · THE SAME THOUGHT

Growing up is not a single moment of maturity. It is the repeated movement from letting others explain the world to observing it for myself. People can offer advice, love, and help, but they cannot live my life for me. The film is playful and suspenseful, using humor to make serious questions approachable. It does not prove every girl can easily escape constraint. Instead, it offers an entry point: Why are some choices still considered unsuitable for women? Why must a girl’s independence be approved by someone else? I love that it gives me not a perfect answer, but the courage to keep asking.

写在最后 · A final note

中文

我写这些，不是因为我已经找到了一套关于女性该怎样生活的标准答案，而是因为这些人物让我更愿意认真想一想：我真正珍惜什么，又有哪些期待其实是别人替我安排的。好的作品不会替我们生活，但会让我们更清醒地回到自己的生活里。

ENGLISH

I am not writing because I have found a standard answer for how women should live. These characters make me think more carefully about what I value and which expectations may have been arranged for me by others. A good work of art cannot live our lives for us, but it can help us return to them with clearer eyes.
