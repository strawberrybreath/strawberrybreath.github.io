HER FILM NOTES / ISSUE 01

Enola Holmes

母亲、女儿，以及成为自己的路Mothers, Daughters, and the Path to Becoming Oneself

我们熟悉 Sherlock Holmes，却未必认识他的妹妹 Enola。比起再讲一遍她如何破案，这次我更想聊聊她和母亲 Eudoria：一个女孩如何带着母亲留下的东西长大，又如何在爱与不解之间，慢慢成为自己。We know Sherlock Holmes, but may know less about his younger sister, Enola. Rather than retelling how she solves cases, this feature looks at Enola and her mother, Eudoria: how a young woman grows with what her mother leaves behind, and becomes herself amid love and misunderstanding.

这篇文章把 Enola Holmes、Enola Holmes 2 与 Enola Holmes 3 放在一起，观察人物关系与主题如何变化。宣传素材不会被误称为剧情证据，作品也不会被当成女性主义的完整答案。This feature considers Enola Holmes, Enola Holmes 2, and Enola Holmes 3 together, tracing how their relationships and themes evolve. Promotional imagery is not treated as narrative evidence, and the films are not presented as complete answers to feminism.

01 / THE FILMS

三部电影：从寻找母亲，到定义自己Three Films: From Finding Her Mother to Defining Herself

三部电影围绕 Enola Holmes 展开：她从母亲失踪后的独自出走开始，逐步成为独立办案的年轻侦探；之后，她面对女性劳动者的处境、制度性的不公，以及亲密关系与个人目标之间的张力。以下按上映顺序列出官方海报与故事简介。The trilogy follows Enola Holmes from her search for her missing mother to her work as an independent young detective. Along the way, she encounters the conditions faced by working women, institutional injustice, and the tension between intimacy and personal ambition. The films are presented below in release order with their official posters and story introductions.

FILM 01 · 2020

Enola Holmes

导演 Harry Bradbeer｜主演 Millie Bobby Brown、Helena Bonham Carter、Henry Cavill

POSTER SOURCE: NETFLIX

剧情概述 / SYNOPSIS

1884年的英格兰，Enola在十六岁生日当天发现母亲 Eudoria 失踪。哥哥们打算把她送进培养“淑女”的寄宿学校，她选择逃往伦敦寻找母亲，并在途中遇到离家出走的年轻贵族 Tewkesbury，随后卷入一场涉及政治改革的阴谋。In 1884 England, Enola discovers that her mother, Eudoria, has disappeared on her sixteenth birthday. When her brothers plan to send her to a finishing school, she escapes to London to find her mother, meets the runaway young lord Tewkesbury, and becomes caught up in a conspiracy involving political reform.

FILM 02 · 2022

Enola Holmes 2

导演 Harry Bradbeer｜主演 Millie Bobby Brown、Henry Cavill、Louis Partridge

POSTER SOURCE: NETFLIX

剧情概述 / SYNOPSIS

第一桩正式委托来自一名火柴女工：她希望Enola找到失踪的姐姐Sarah。调查将Enola带入伦敦火柴工厂及其相关人际网络，案件也逐步牵出更大的阴谋；她需要朋友与Sherlock的帮助才能继续查明真相。Enola’s first official case comes from a match factory worker who asks her to find her missing sister, Sarah. The investigation leads into the London match factory and its network of relationships, gradually uncovering a larger conspiracy. Enola needs help from friends and Sherlock to solve the case.

02 / MOTHER & DAUGHTER

爱与分歧：母女如何重新看见彼此Love and Conflict: Learning to See Each Other

“EUDORIA TO ENOLA · 2022
太多人把人生唯一的目标变成融入周围的世界。这是个错误。“Too many people make it their sole purpose in life to fit into the world around them. This is a mistake.”
ENOLA HOLMES 2 · 2022拒绝被规训 / RESIST CONFORMITY

情节注释 / SCENE NOTE这句台词来自第二部中Eudoria与Enola谈及社会规范的对话。她提醒女儿，为了被世界接纳而不断改变自己，并不等于真正活得自由。母亲的理想与女儿因她离开而承受的失落，也因此同时存在。In the second film, Eudoria speaks with Enola about social expectations. She reminds her daughter that reshaping oneself to fit the world is not the same as living freely. Her ideals and the hurt caused by her absence both remain part of their relationship.

Enola和母亲Eudoria彼此深爱，却并不总能理解对方。母亲选择离开，投身她认为重要的事业；Enola则被留下来，独自面对失踪带来的困惑，也不得不重新认识那个自己以为很熟悉的母亲。对Enola来说，寻找母亲既是一场侦探冒险，也是在追问：我在她的人生里究竟意味着什么？Enola and her mother, Eudoria, love each other deeply, but they do not always understand one another. Eudoria leaves to pursue a cause she believes in; Enola is left with uncertainty and must reconsider the mother she thought she knew. For Enola, finding her mother is both a detective adventure and a personal question: what place do I have in her life?

电影没有把母亲写成一个永远正确、无所不能的榜样。Eudoria有自己的理想，也有她没有向女儿解释清楚的决定。她希望Enola拥有自由，却未必总能意识到，突然失去母亲的陪伴，也会让女儿感到受伤。她们的矛盾不是简单的谁对谁错，而是两个人都在争取改变世界的空间，却还需要学习怎样彼此倾听。The film does not make Eudoria a flawless, all-knowing role model. She has ideals of her own and makes choices she does not fully explain to her daughter. She wants Enola to be free, yet does not always recognise how painful her absence can be. Their conflict is not simply a matter of right and wrong: both are trying to make room to change the world, and both still need to learn how to listen to each other.

这也是母女关系里很真实的一面：爱并不会自动带来理解，亲密也不意味着一个人可以替另一个人决定。母亲可以有自己的事业和信念，女儿也可以表达失望、提出问题，而不必因此被视为不懂事。两个人不需要完全相同，关系才算成功；有时，真正的靠近，是愿意承认彼此的差异，并且为自己的选择负责。This is one of the truths of mother-daughter relationships: love does not automatically create understanding, and closeness does not give one person the right to decide for another. A mother can have her own work and convictions; a daughter can voice disappointment and ask difficult questions without being dismissed as immature. They do not have to become alike for their relationship to matter. Sometimes, getting closer begins with acknowledging their differences and taking responsibility for their choices.

所以，Enola和Eudoria之间最动人的地方，不是她们终于变成了完全相同的人，而是她们逐渐看见：对方也有自己的恐惧、目标和边界。母亲不能替女儿走完人生，女儿也不必靠否定母亲来证明独立。爱可以成为支持，却不应该成为控制；理解值得努力，但不应被要求成无条件的服从。What makes their relationship moving is not that they become the same, but that they begin to see each other’s fears, ambitions, and boundaries. A mother cannot live her daughter’s life for her, and a daughter need not reject her mother to prove her independence. Love can offer support without becoming control. Understanding is worth working toward, but it should never be demanded as unconditional obedience.

03 / GROWING UP

成长不是复制，而是做出自己的选择Growing Up Means Choosing for Yourself

“EDITORIAL NOTE · NOT DIALOGUE
编辑概括：成长不只是离开别人安排的道路，也包括辨认自己真正想走向哪里。Editorial paraphrase: Growing up means not only leaving a path chosen by others, but recognising where you genuinely want to go.
EDITORIAL PARAPHRASE · NOT A FILM QUOTE选择权 / AGENCY

情节注释 / SCENE NOTE这张卡不是电影原句，而是对第一部开场冲突的编辑概括：Mycroft准备把Enola送进寄宿学校，Enola则追随母亲留下的线索逃往伦敦。电影用两种相反的行动呈现冲突：一方要把她安置进既定的女性角色，另一方坚持亲自调查、亲自判断。This card is an editorial paraphrase, not dialogue. In the first film, Mycroft plans to send Enola to a finishing school; she instead follows her mother’s clues and escapes to London. The conflict is shown through opposing actions: one side tries to fit her into a prescribed feminine role, while Enola insists on investigating for herself.

Enola从母亲那里继承了很多东西，却不必活成另一个 Eudoria。她可以学习母亲的勇气，也可以质疑母亲的选择；可以珍惜母亲留下的线索，也可以走向一条母亲没有走过的路。Enola inherits much from Eudoria without having to become her. She can learn from her mother’s courage and question her decisions; she can value what her mother leaves behind while taking a road her mother never travelled.

这也是女性成长很重要的一部分：我们可以受他人影响，但不必把任何人的人生当作标准答案。独立并不意味着永远不需要别人，而是即使爱着别人，也仍然有权参与决定自己的生活。We can be influenced by others without treating anyone’s life as the model answer. Independence does not mean never needing anyone; it means retaining a say in our own lives, even when we love other people.

电影里的 Enola 拥有机敏、行动力和幽默感，但她不是必须被复制的“正确女性”。不是每个人都要像她一样冒险，也不是所有人都必须用强硬的方式表达自己。一个人可以谨慎、可以寻求帮助，也可以暂时没有答案；重要的是，她的选择不应被默认剥夺。Enola is quick-witted, active, and funny, but she is not a “correct woman” everyone must imitate. Not everyone needs to take the same risks or express themselves forcefully. A person can be cautious, ask for help, or have no answer yet; what matters is that her ability to choose is not taken away by default.

谈论女性独立时，也要小心不要把结构问题变成个人作业。并非每个人都拥有相同的经济条件、家庭支持与社会空间。尊重自主，也包括看见限制，不把困境简单归咎于一个人不够努力。When discussing women’s independence, we should not turn structural problems into individual homework. People do not have equal access to money, family support, or social space. Respecting agency also means recognising constraints instead of blaming hardship on a lack of effort.

04 / WHO DEFINES HER?

谁来决定女孩应该成为什么样的人？Who Decides Who a Girl Should Become?

“EUDORIA TO ENOLA · 2022
如果你想让别人听见你的声音，就得发出自己的声音。“You have to make some noise if you want to be heard.”
ENOLA HOLMES 2 · 2022发出声音 / BE HEARD

情节注释 / SCENE NOTE这句台词出现在第二部母女重逢后的对话中。Eudoria向Enola解释自己离开的原因，并承认自己曾希望改变女儿将要面对的世界。台词谈“发声”，但场景并未抹去Enola被留下的失落：母亲的政治理想与女儿的情感经验必须同时被看见。The line appears in the conversation after mother and daughter reunite in the second film. Eudoria explains why she left and acknowledges the world she hoped to change for her daughter. The line is about speaking up, but the scene does not erase Enola’s hurt at being left behind: her mother’s political ideals and her daughter’s emotional experience both matter.

女性主义不只是把女性角色放在故事中央。更重要的是，作品是否允许她们拥有自己的动机、矛盾、判断和后果，是否承认她们不只是推动其他人物成长的工具。A feminist reading does more than place a woman at the centre of a story. It asks whether she is allowed her own motives, contradictions, judgement, and consequences—and whether she exists as more than a tool for someone else’s development.

Enola Holmes用轻快的节奏讲述冒险，也留下值得讨论的问题：谁有权决定一个女孩应该成为什么样的人？当母亲与女儿都试图改变世界，她们能否既相爱，又保留各自的道路？Enola Holmes tells an adventure with a lively rhythm, while leaving questions worth discussing: who gets to decide what a girl should become? When mother and daughter both seek to change the world, can they love each other and still keep their own paths?

电影不需要替我们回答所有问题。它能做的，是把问题放到眼前，让我们重新看见那些曾被当作理所当然的规则。A film need not answer every question for us. It can bring a question into view and help us notice rules we once accepted as natural.

这套电影把一个并不轻盈的问题包裹在冒险之中：当社会早已替你写好角色说明书，你能不能拿回来，自己修改？Enola有时冲动，有时聪明，也会犯错；她不是因为从不失手才值得被看见，而是因为故事允许她继续行动、继续判断。The films wrap a serious question inside an adventure: when society has already written your role, can you take the script back and revise it? Enola can be impulsive, clever, and mistaken. She matters not because she never fails, but because the story lets her keep acting and judging for herself.

05 / TONE & LIMITS

轻快的冒险，也能承载严肃的议题A Light Adventure Can Carry Serious Questions

喜剧感、快速推进的冒险和 Enola 与观众直接交流的方式，让观众可以轻松进入故事。轻快的形式并不会自动削弱议题；有时它反而让关于权力、性别角色和社会期待的问题更容易被看见。Comedy, fast-moving adventure, and Enola’s direct address to the audience make the story easy to enter. A light form does not automatically weaken its themes; at times, it can make questions of power, gender roles, and social expectation easier to see.

但我们不必把它当成女性主义的完整答案。它仍是一部商业类型片，有自己的叙事取舍与浪漫化表达。Enola的好奇心、行动力，以及她与母亲之间并不简单的关系，足够成为继续思考的起点。Still, the films are not complete answers to feminism. They remain commercial genre films, with narrative compromises and romanticised elements. Enola’s curiosity, initiative, and complicated relationship with her mother offer a starting point for further thought.

“ENOLA · CLOSING VOICE
我的人生由我自己做主。未来掌握在我们手中。“My life is my own. And the future is up to us.”
ENOLA HOLMES · 2020自主与未来 / AGENCY & FUTURE

情节注释 / SCENE NOTE这句话是第一部结尾由Enola说出的收束性表达，不是Eudoria的台词。镜头让她直接面向观众回顾自己的选择，把私人经历延伸为面向其他女性的邀请。“我们”也很重要：影片让个人选择与共同改变未来并置。Enola speaks these closing words in the first film; they are not Eudoria’s dialogue. By addressing the audience directly, she turns her personal choices into an invitation to other women. The word “us” matters: the film places individual agency alongside the possibility of changing the future together.

06 / CLOSING NOTE

独立不是孤身一人Independence Does Not Mean Being Alone

我喜欢 Enola 身上那种机敏、幽默和不愿轻易服从的劲头，也喜欢这个故事没有要求她必须在母亲、爱情、家庭和自我之间交出一个唯一正确的答案。I admire Enola’s wit, humour, and reluctance to obey without question. I also value that the story does not demand one perfect answer from her about her mother, romance, family, or herself.

或许长大不是终于变成一个无所不能的人，而是慢慢知道：我可以爱我的母亲，也可以与她不同；我可以听取别人的意见，也可以自己做决定；我可以在寻找答案的路上不断改变。Perhaps growing up does not mean becoming capable of everything. It means learning that I can love my mother and still differ from her; listen to others and still make my own decisions; and keep changing as I search for answers.
